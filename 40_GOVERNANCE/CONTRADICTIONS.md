# THE CARRIED CONTRADICTIONS

> *"Where his words conflict across time, the LATER doctrine supersedes and a supersession note says
> so. Where the resolution is a coder's reading rather than his own later words, the note says THAT...
> **Nothing is silently blended.**"* — the estate's standing handling rule

> *"Only he retires a line. A coder note is marked as a coder note and retires nothing."*

**What this file is:** every place two things in the system disagree, held open where he has not
closed them. **It is not a bug list to be cleared.** A contradiction inside his own doctrine is a
founder-decision class; a contradiction between two coder documents is a coder's to resolve and say so.

**How to read the columns:** *His* means both sides are his words. *Coder* means at least one side is
a coder's assembly. **The two are handled differently and must never be merged into one queue.**

Mt Rushmore puts them first, in "doctrine night." His 4PM spec speaks them after the worlds. The Great

### A-4 — "The work creates the wonder" vs "the wonder creates the work"

working reading, sourced and marked, **never quoted as his.**

**Why this matters and is not philosophy:** the reversed reading is what lets a coder point at a route
file, a class, or a cron and call cold code a wonder. Whichever direction he rules, that loophole
stays closed.

### A-8 — Mount Rushmore: five faces, seven, or six?

### A-9 — Commandments 6 through 10 are unruled

### A-10 — Equity: two passes that do not reconcile

### A-11 — Is the researcher born?

## SECTION D — THE CONTAMINATED TEST

**The park name Easter egg is spent unless he reseeds it.** Later sessions read the monuments aloud by
name, so the answer is now in the corpus. A correct answer proves retrieval, not inference.

The lane that noticed this flagged it rather than letting a future session claim the pass. **That
handling is the standard**: when a test is contaminated, say so — a passed test that proves nothing is
worse than a failed one, because it ends the investigation.

## HOW A NEW CONTRADICTION ENTERS THIS FILE

1. Both sides quoted, with sources.
2. Classified **His** or **Coder**.
3. If his: routed to him, **nothing built on either reading** until he rules, and any working posture
   labelled as a working posture with a named author who can be overruled.
4. If a coder's: resolved, with the resolution and its reasoning written down — and if the resolution
   is a measurement (curl it, count it, run the test), **take the measurement instead of arguing.**
5. Never blended. Never quietly dropped when it becomes inconvenient.
