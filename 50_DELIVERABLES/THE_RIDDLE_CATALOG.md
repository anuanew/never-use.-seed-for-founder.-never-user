## HOW TO READ THIS

He asked for **three columns**, not four. The third one is an **or**, and the or is the whole point:

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **1. WHAT HE SAID** | His words, verbatim. Cussing rendered `[expletive]`. Speech-to-text garble carried raw, with the reading in square brackets after it. |
| **2. WHAT THE CODER THOUGHT THE BLANK WAS** | The first, smaller conclusion. Where recorded. Where no coder ever wrote one down, it says so. |
| **3. WHAT HE CONFIRMED IT TO BE, **OR** WHAT THE CODER JUST ABSOLUTELY KNEW IT WAS** | One of two, and **every entry is stamped with which one.** |

The third column carries one of three stamps:

- **`CONFIRMED BY HIM`** - he said the answer himself, in the same breath, in a later doctrine, or in
  a standing law that is quoted here with its file. The receipt is his own mouth.
- **`JUST KNEW IT`** - no confirmation from him exists anywhere in this corpus. A coder read it and
  was sure. **This is the column where bad reads hide, and it is exactly why he asked for it.** Every
  one of these is a claim on his meaning that he has never signed off on.
- **`OPEN`** - he asked and deliberately did not answer. **These are not failures.** They stay open.

### Method and honesty rules this file was built under

- Nothing inside a quotation mark was altered, cleaned, or de-garbled. Where the transcription
  mangled a word, the raw form is carried and the reading follows in `[brackets]`.
- Where a line sits on a generic `Speaker N` label rather than the founder's own named speaker label,
  it says so on the line and is marked `(no writer stamp on the line)`. It is not attributed to him.
- No personal names of private individuals appear. Roles are bracketed: `[a partner]`, `[a pastor]`,
  `[a relative]`. Where a private name is the literal subject of a riddle, the name is redacted inside
  the quote as `[a name]` and flagged on the spot. He knows the name; the catalog does not need it.
- No credentials, keys, tokens, or world ids.
- Every claim is stamped `[HIS WORDS + file]` or `[CODER'S READING]`.
