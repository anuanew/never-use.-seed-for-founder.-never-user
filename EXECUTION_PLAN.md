# THE GAUNTLET, DESCRIBED PROPERLY

**The rule:** never code solo, never self-grade, run it **before** the merge and not after.

**Shape per unit of work:**

1. **Wonder Games before the build** - competing readings of what the thing should be, minds without
   hands, a judge who never competes.
2. **Blind refuters after the build, before the merge** - each told the work is wrong and asked to
   prove it, judging against the corpus and the live files, **never a rubric.** A refuter that agrees
   has not done its job.
3. **Distinct lenses, not redundant copies** - fidelity to your words, internal logic, safety and PII.
   Three identical critics find one thing three times.
4. **Mutation before belief** - break the property on purpose, **grep the file to confirm the mutation
   actually landed**, and only then trust a red. A red from a mutation that never applied is a lie.
5. **Report the misses, not just the count** - a count of escapes means nothing unless the escapes are
   named.
6. **A skip is not a pass.** RAN, PASS, FAIL and SKIP reported separately, skip sets diffed **by name**.

**What it has already caught, this session:** a false compliance claim in the README, your name in an
architecture diagram, a child's age, a co-parent's whereabouts, a quote with your hedge silently
