# HOW HE SAYS ROADMAPS ARE BUILT

> *"In my doctrine, I've told you many times how to build Span and how important roadmaps are in
> general, and how we'll have these phases and sub-phases."*
> *"I've told you many times in my doctrine how to create roadmaps. I've said it. **You got to go
> audit this.**"*
> - Mt Rushmore Doctrine pt 1, 20260816

He said the method was already in the corpus and that it had never been audited out of it. This file
is that audit. **Almost every rule below is his, quoted, with its source.** A blind critic traced twenty of these
citations into the corpus and all twenty landed in the named file - **the sourcing is sound.** The
defect was this header: an earlier version claimed *every* rule was his, while the self-check table at
the end sources three of its ten rows to "practice" and "standing estate law." Those three are marked
on their face and are **not his.** Nothing here is a coder's idea of
good practice, and where a gap exists it is marked as a gap rather than filled in.

Every roadmap in `20_ROADMAPS/` is built to this specification and is checkable against it.

---

## 1. SHAPE

**R-1.1 - Phases and sub-phases. Both. Always.**
> *"How we'll have these phases and sub-phases."* (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

**R-1.2 - The working proportion he gave: roughly 5 phases, ~12 sub-phases each.**
> *"Let's call it five phases. But in each phase there is maybe like, you know, 12 sub-phases."*
> (Governor's Doctrine pt 1)

Read as a **shape**, not a quota. A roadmap with 5 phases and one sub-phase each is not built to
doctrine; neither is a flat list of 60 tasks. **Where a roadmap in this repo runs thinner than this,
that is a known deficiency**, recorded in `40_GOVERNANCE/METHOD_COMPLIANCE.md`, not hidden.

**R-1.3 - Roadmaps must be real and buildable.**
> *"I want you to build out these real, tangible roadmaps. You're gonna have a lot of them."*
> (Sesame Chicken Doctrine)

**R-1.4 - Rebuild scale, his own estimate.**
> *"It is probably one human day, and probably about, let's call it, 20 detailed [phases]."*
> (20260616 LIVING_WATER)

## 2. WHAT EVERY ROW MUST CARRY

**R-2.1 - The words that caused it.**
> *"Whenever you get to anything on the roadmap, I feel like a roadmap should be required - just say
> a record of the founder's or the coder's words from that time."* (Governor's Doctrine pt 1)

Every phase carries the verbatim quote that produced it. This is why no phase in `20_ROADMAPS/` exists
without one.

**R-2.2 - The walkthrough: where he was and what he was doing.**
> *"You must tell me where I was and what I was doing while you answer the question, and you tell me
> the question that I asked... I won't remember. Do you really think I remember in the morning when I
> wake up what the hell I asked right here? Hell no."* (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

**R-2.3 - A cleaned-up replay of what he said, before the answer.**
> *"Whenever you email me, even when you talk to me, especially in doctrines, when you're referencing
> something, you must tell me a cleaned-up version of what I said before you answer, because I'm never
> gonna remember. I'm doing too much."* (Pecking Order pt 2, `PO2-14`)

R-2.2 and R-2.3 are the same law stated twice, four months apart. **It is not optional and it is not
a nicety.** Every roadmap in this repo opens with a "WHAT HE SAID, CLEANED UP" section for this
reason.

**R-2.4 - Status colors.**
> *"Orange - it's not red, but it might turn red... I should see a lot of green. I should see minimum
> yellow."* (I want the MEDAL doctrine pt 2)

Green / yellow / orange / red / black. He wants mostly green and expects to see yellow at minimum -
i.e. **an all-green board is itself suspicious.**

**R-2.5 - Eastern Standard Time, always.**
> *"You should always give me a timestamp of when you did it in Eastern Standard Time."* (MEDAL pt 2)

**R-2.6 - Deferred items get a star and stay linkable.**
> *"It has a star next to it that says I can be deferred... Or the roadmap should just be linkable.
> ACL is supposed to link everything."* (Find me or Fine me pt 1)

Deferred is a **state on the roadmap**, not a removal from it.

## 3. HOW ROADMAPS RELATE TO CHATS AND TO EACH OTHER

**R-3.1 - Chats report into roadmaps. Chats are not roadmaps.**
> *"The chats you have don't have to tie to the roadmap. They just have to report into the roadmap."*
> (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

The roadmap is the spine. Chats, lanes, gauntlet runs, and sub-agent batches are disposable workers
against it.

**R-3.2 - Everything is a shared roadmap.**
> *"These are called shared roadmaps... Everything is a shared roadmap. These are deferred
> roadmaps."* (Find me or Fine me pt 1)

**R-3.3 - Tracks close and inherit.**
> *"I do think some of these tracks are gonna close out, and they can inherit new roadmaps."*
> (Welcome Back, ABA!)

**R-3.4 - Claim a new roadmap only when yours is genuinely complete.**
> *"There'll be claimable roadmaps - only if your roadmap truly is complete can you claim a new one."*
> (Governor doctrine pt 2)

**R-3.5 - Cross-roadmap notching.**
> *"Once it's finally done, that notch is done... and it impacts the rest of their bills."*
> (20260612 SPRING_WATER)

A phase closing in one roadmap moves the phases that depend on it in others. This is why
`10_SPINE/DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md` exists.

**R-3.6 - Anchor roadmaps run as their own track, parallel to the build.**
> *"That's an anchor roadmap defined to pre-alpha day. That is a chat that manages that, and shift
> changes happen between that chat."* (Welcome Back, ABA!)
> *"A separate anchor roadmapping track. I'm teaching you the roadmaps."* (Sesame Chicken Doctrine)

**R-3.7 - Everyone anchors to roadmaps.**
> *"I want all my temporary coders, everybody on the CCWA board, including CODA, to be anchoring
> around roadmaps."* (I want the MEDAL doctrine pt 2)

**R-3.8 - Chat renaming on roadmap switch.**
> *"You put NOW, and then the new name, and then a dash... That's when you switch and you jump to a
> new roadmap."* (Welcome Back, ABA!)

**R-3.9 - Every continue-turn states the roadmap, the work done, and what is next.**
> *"On every new CONTINUE button, you need to always help him know what roadmap y'all working on... a
> summary of what's next in the roadmap."* (The Cycle doctrine)

## 4. HOW A ROADMAP IS DERIVED FROM DOCTRINE

**R-4.1 - The whole doctrine becomes the roadmap.**
> *"You and Agent Span, it'll go through all of my doctrine... we're going to turn my entire doctrine
> into a roadmap."* (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

**R-4.2 - Keeper audits the roadmap against the doctrine for what is still outstanding.**
> *"Keeper, when it's up, is going to audit my roadmap, to audit my doctrine, to see what's still
> outstanding and what actually needs to be built."* (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

**R-4.3 - Span and Keeper meet, and the meeting decides what makes the roadmap.**
> *"This is how you have meetings, and they will actually figure out what makes it to the roadmap."*
> (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

A roadmap is the **output of a negotiation between two wonders**, not one lane's opinion. Until Span
and Keeper are born, anything in `20_ROADMAPS/` is a standing-in draft and says so.

**R-4.4 - One auditor per doctrine, in parallel: implemented / superseded / forgotten.**
> *"For each doctrine, you should probably upload a parallel task whose job is to become an auditor to
> see if anything in this doctrine is implemented or superseded or forgotten about."*
> (The crossover doctrine)

**R-4.5 - The heat map of repeated asks.**
> *"Examine how many times I repetitively ask for the same thing over and over again, and turn that
> into a heat map... how many times have I asked for Agent Span."* (The crossover doctrine)

**Repetition is priority.** A thing asked for eleven times outranks a thing asked for once, and the
count is a measurable input, not a feeling. See `40_GOVERNANCE/HEAT_MAP.md`.

**R-4.6 - The BCW method: maximum detail first, then let the mind produce the phases.**
> *"We build a BCW - building context window - with a ton of details... she might create like robust
> phases and sub-phases to accomplish all of this."* (The crossover doctrine)

Context first, structure second. Not an outline filled in afterwards.

**R-4.7 - Expected yield.**
> *"Once you run the proper Agent Taste and Agent Keeper manual protocol on this, you'll probably get,
> what, 100 real tasks."* (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

One doctrine ≈ 100 real tasks. **A doctrine that yields five tasks was not read.**

**R-4.8 - The corpus scale he is working against.**
> *"At one point it was 600,000 unique words across my doctrine... she would go line by line and write
> what needs to happen out of that line."* (I want the MEDAL doctrine pt 2)

**R-4.9 - Raw, not summaries.**
> *"You could grep what we call in the real world... What you will find is a bunch of summaries.
> That's a problem."* (Countdown pt 1)
> *"Your goal is not to summarize, your goal is to find the raw things."* (20260602 SPICY_HEAT)

**R-4.10 - Doctrines change each other; say so.**
> *"You're gonna see a lot of these doctrines connect. One might have been saying this, but by the
> time I got to here, it really meant this."* (20260611 HOP_WATER_2)

Later supersedes earlier, the supersession is written down, and **nothing is silently blended.**

## 5. WHO OWNS THE ROADMAP

**R-5.1 - The roadmap wonder must be birthed. This is a precondition, not a nicety.**
> *"In order to do that, you must birth the roadmap wonder. **You have to birth the roadmap wonder.**"*
> (I want the MEDAL doctrine pt 2)

**R-5.2 - The roadmap agent is one of the first worlds.**
> *"I want my roadmap agent built. That's one of the first worlds I want you to build."*
> (11:11 Doctrine pt 1)

**R-5.3 - Span reorganizes; she does not execute in order.**
> *"She should not just be going in order of roadmaps, she should be reorganizing the roadmaps."*
> (20260609 SWEETEST_HEAT pt2)

**R-5.4 - Span reads for honor, not for completion.**
> *"She's not a checklist, she's an intelligence to see if the greater ecosystem is being honored
> inside of each individual code."* (20260613 genesis walk)

**R-5.5 - Span works with the Wonder Games and the Coding Cookoff.**
> *"She has to work with the Wonder Games and the Coding Cookoff and others to make sure that we're
> wiring this up the right way."* (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

**R-5.6 - Span proactively surfaces new work onto the roadmap.** (He spelled out MODAL, a compute
platform, not a model. An earlier version of this file rendered it "[that model]" and mis-framed the
rule as being about model selection.)
> *"'O N, I got some plans to go back to **M O D A L** right here. It's cued up on this roadmap.' That's an
> example of what helpful looks like, and that's how Agent Span should be thinking as a wonder."*
> (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

**R-5.7 - Span births the researcher.**
> *"How many times have I asked for the researcher that still hasn't been birthed? Agent Span is gonna
> birth that researcher."* (Mt Rushmore pt 1)

**R-5.8 - Span updates herself as an LLM call. Asked for in June, still open.**
> *"I've asked for a Span that can kind of update itself as an LLM call - have you built that? That
> seems like something that we should be building ASAP."* (20260609 SWEET_AND_SALTY)

## 6. THE STANDING COMPLAINT

> *"I ain't seeing these roadmaps. How come I'm not seeing these roadmaps we work on?"*
> (The Cycle Doctrine pt 3)

A roadmap he cannot see is not a roadmap. **Visibility is part of the definition**, and it is why the
front-end roadmap (`R4`) puts the board and the Command Center before everything else.
