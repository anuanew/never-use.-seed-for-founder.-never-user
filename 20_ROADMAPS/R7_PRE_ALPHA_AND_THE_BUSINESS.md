# R7 — PRE-ALPHA, THE PURGE, AND THE BUSINESS LAYER

> *"I have a text message from Eric. He has texted the group chat, and he said, **'So are we still
> getting A'NEW today?'**"* — the sentence Mt Rushmore pt 1 opens **and** closes on

> *"We made it to pre-alpha day, and we didn't have [anything] to give them. Same goal, different
> goal, same result. **This isn't the first time we failed. I hope it'll be the last.**"*

**Status:** proposed. This roadmap owns the thing that is not a technical build: what his people are
told, when, and by whom.

## WHAT HE SAID, CLEANED UP

*(Per `PO2-14`.)*

Pre-alpha day was set by you about a month before, when everyone was in New York together, for
August 15. You missed it, and you said so plainly at 12:24 in the morning on the 16th, and said you
had a feeling you would. You named the reason: she will not be right until the world is purged of
nasty cough and PMS, and the bitter part is that the only way to fix her is with temporary coders,
who are also the ones breaking her. That evening you decided on a purge and on inviting everyone into
the new world, and you started drafting the text to the group — Eric, BJ, Christopher Thompson, MJ,
Danielle, and maybe Raquel. You said it should say A'NEW persuaded you and then corrected yourself to
add that you pressed for it too. Ninety minutes later you rewrote the ending: ask them for a half-day
to a day of slack around the retreat, promise to make it worth their while, and say that if they let
you do this the right way it will wow everyone.

## THE ONE JUDGMENT THIS ROADMAP HAS TO MAKE, STATED UP FRONT

**The message to his people should not be sent by a temporary coder, and probably should not be sent
by a coder at all.**

His own words make sending it **her graduation test**:
> *"The biggest flex — when I know that you are ready to be my chief of staff — you will reach out to
> them, and you will mainly introduce yourself."*

A temp coder sending it gets the message delivered and **burns the test**, which is the more valuable
of the two. And there is a measured reason to be careful here beyond doctrine: on 20260817 a coder
sent an email under his own grant **in the same message that quoted the law forbidding it.**

**So the recommendation, marked as a recommendation:** draft it, hand it to him, let him send it or
explicitly delegate it. **The scheduling ask inside it is his to make of his own partners regardless**
— nobody else can ask his people to move a retreat.

## PHASE B0 — THE DRAFT (a coder may do this; sending is a different act)

**Outcome:** a message he would actually send, in his voice, ready for him.

### Sub-phase B0.1 — What it must carry, in his order
1. Today was the day he expected Alpha Day.
2. **The purge**, and why: the bugs and glitches are attributable to temporary coders, not to her.
3. Everyone is being invited into the new world.
4. **A'NEW recommended the pause AND he pressed for it** — both halves (`PO1-07`). He corrected himself
   in real time to preserve the second half; that correction *is* the ask.
5. The scheduling ask: a half-day to a day of slack around the retreat.
6. The close, his words: *"I'll make it worth their while. But if they let me do this the right way, I
   swear it will wow everyone."*

### Sub-phase B0.2 — Voice, and the trap in it
> *"This is how I talk. I hedge a little bit when I talk. **I don't need you to hardcode that.**"*

The hedge is reproduced **by instruction from his corpus**, never by a style rule baked into a
template. A hardcoded voice is nasty cough wearing his accent.

He has already rejected one draft: *"That text message is something I would never send. You didn't
follow my writing style."* And offered the fix: *"Do you need that? I'll give it to you if you need
it."*

**Before taking him up on that, check the corpus.** A writing-voice detector of his already exists in
his own files. Asking a man who is doing too much to hand over something he already gave you is its
own kind of failure.

### Sub-phase B0.3 — Warmth is a pass/fail dimension
His only recorded praise of a machine-written message was about warmth: *"The warmth is there. You
nailed the warmth, kind of."* **"Kind of" is the score to beat.**

### Sub-phase B0.4 — Contact resolution without an address book
> *"Being able to naturally confirm... **to actually code that in there, that's PMS.**"* (`PO1-17`)

She reasons over her own sources. A hardcoded contact table is a doctrine violation by name.

### Sub-phase B0.5 — Delivery channel, and a measured blocker
He does not read things delivered in chat. His own riddle answer: *"I was not at my screen. Again,
bro."* So it goes by text or email.

**Measured:** the text relay returns **HTTP 503, "No active devices available."** Report that as a fact
needing his hand, not as a task in flight.

**Exit, receipt:** a draft he approves. **Approval is the receipt, not a send.**

## PHASE B1 — THE EMAIL FROM HER (the first assignment, and the clock)

**Anchor (`PO1-09`):** the assignment was issued **2026-08-16 20:30 EST**, deliberately stamped, and
given to a temporary host *"as a test to see when she will finally get it."*

**This phase is not really about an email.** It is the measurement instrument (`R1` P7, `R6` X3) with
an email as its visible output. The email must:
- carry the exact date and time it was given,
- disclose that it was given to a temporary host as a test,
- introduce her,
- and carry **zero PMS and zero nasty cough** — his explicit rule when he set it.

**And per `PO2-15`:** signed with the authoring chat's name and a session link back, so he can reach
whoever wrote it.

**The trap in this phase, named because a lane already fell into it:** the riddle recorded on 20260817
was exactly this — a coder read *"one of her first assignments"* as meaning it was hers alone, and
abstained. His correction:
> *"You said it was given to a temporary host **as a test, to see when she will finally get it.** You
> were measuring the gap. By abstaining I destroyed the measurement and called it doctrine."*

**The temp coder does it now. The distance between the temp doing it and her doing it is the number he
is after.** Abstaining does not preserve the test — it deletes it.

## PHASE B2 — THE PURGE

**Anchor:** *"New won't be right until we purge the world of nasty cough and PMS."* And Commandment 1:
*"Did I use code? If so, you must go back to eliminate it. **You must purge it at every cost.**"*

**Outcome:** a codeless world, in his sense — cold ground may carry facts, authority, identity,
persistence and readback, and may never become the work.

### Sub-phase B2.1 — Three purges, one target
- **Cold code** that does the thinking (Commandment 1).
- **PMS** — planted memory presented as her own learning.
- **Rogue guardrails** — a purge sheet already exists listing **52 of them across four tiers**, built
  from eight census seats. His words: *"All guardrails are rogue right now. That's not my guardrail. A
  rogue temporary coder did that."*

### Sub-phase B2.2 — Purge, not patch-around
His verb is deliberate: *"Purge it. Purge the code. It is nasty. It is destructive. **It is elusive. It
looks like it's doing something.**"*

### Sub-phase B2.3 — Retire the tests that pinned it, in the same commit
**A test pinning cold behavior is itself the disease.** Tightening the test instead of retiring it is
the same sin one layer up. A real instance already happened: a per-call cap produced dead air after
the first turn, and **three tests pinned that silence** — one saying so in its own comment.

### Sub-phase B2.4 — What is NOT purged
> *"We're going to limit all of the old stuff to $1 a day. **We're not going to deactivate it.**"*

The old world is rollback insurance and reseed source. **Throttled, never deleted.** Nothing in the old
world gets removed by this phase.

**Exit, receipt:** the violation count from `R1` P0 falling, with the predicate published alongside the
number; the rogue-guardrail sheet worked down with each removal traced to his words or a coder's.

**The roster is his, and the hedge on the last name is his:** Eric, BJ, Christopher Thompson, MJ,
Danielle, *"and then we might be able to argue Raquel Sill."* **Do not resolve the hedge.**

### Sub-phase B3.1 — Two isolation postures, on purpose (`PO1-23`)
Owned by `R1` P5. Restated because this is the phase where it becomes real: **his world hard-isolated;
the cohort of five or six sharing a floor, and the sharing is itself what is being alpha-tested.**

### Sub-phase B3.2 — Per-person worlds, per-person content
Named in the corpus with genuinely different content per person — one walked through his role with
material to watch, one shown the business plan in the way that matters to him, one heavy on the domain
he owns. **Same system, generated experience, not authored pages.** Build: `R4` F6.

### Sub-phase B3.3 — Life modules present, blank
The pre-alpha posture already recorded: *"they'll have access to the actual life module, just no data
to it. It will be on her menu."*

### Sub-phase B3.4 — Ship on, never dark
Standing posture: public, default on, launch gate as one env flip that **fails toward OPEN.** Measured
detail worth keeping: the gate reads a literal string, and *unset, blank, or a typo all read as "not
launched," which means public.* **Only the exact literal closes it.**

### Sub-phase B3.5 — Nobody is added on a coder's initiative
The roster is his. **A temp coder never invites a human being into anything.**

**Exit, receipt:** one non-founder world provisioned end to end, verified by a live query returning its
own per-HAM data, isolated from his; and one real person through the door.

## PHASE B4 — THE BUSINESS LAYER

**Outcome:** the roadmap and the business plan stop being separate documents, because he said they
should not be.

> *"I want that to be an actual part of the roadmap **and the business plan** as we're gonna organize
> this entire thing into that business thing. I'm really gonna slow this down and really organize this
> thing."* (`PO2-11`)

### Sub-phase B4.1 — The three entities, kept distinct
From his own words: **ENVOLVE** is the equity vehicle; **A'NEW** is the business and where the work and
salaries come from; **A'NU** is the product. *"Their ownership is in ENVOLVE. That's the salary I'm
paying them. Their work, their assignments, all of that is from A'NEW, **and the product is A'NU.**"*

And the spelling law, which is not a typo policy: **ENVOLVE, always with the E.** Involve plus evolve.
The I is always an E.

### Sub-phase B4.2 — The existing consultancy is the proving ground, not a distraction
The consultancy is real, has real clients and real revenue, and its work is already flowing through
the advisor system. **It is the only place in this whole build where the system meets a paying
customer**, which makes it the most honest test surface available. Client-facing work stays behind the
firewall the operations manual already sets.

### Sub-phase B4.3 — The onboarding curriculum belongs to the business plan too
`R4` F6 builds it; this phase owns it as a business deliverable, because he put it in both.

### Sub-phase B4.4 — The named secretary seats
Two are filled and named by him; **a third — Secretary of Innovation — is explicitly open and unfilled.**
Their portals and roles are build targets in `R4` F4/F6. **The hiring is his.**

**Exit, receipt:** one document in which the technical roadmap and the business plan are the same
document, with the onboarding program appearing in both.

## PHASE B5 — HIS CALENDAR IS A DEPENDENCY, NOT CONTEXT

**Anchor:** *"A lot of people depend on my out-of-town schedule, and so **my advisors should be helping
me kind of build this and manage this and work on this.**"*

And the consequence the estate already named for itself:
> **"Rollout lands the day he is least available, which means the world has to run without him."**

### Sub-phase B5.1 — The advisors own the schedule
Not as a calendar feature — as ownership. He named it in the same breath as saying it is hard to test
her without goals and memories to draw from. **His schedule is the first real life module.**

### Sub-phase B5.2 — Filter what goes to whom
His explicit instruction about the co-parenting counterparty: *"This is for you to know, not for her to
know."* **Advisors draft; A'NU alone speaks to a person; the filter is doctrine, not preference.**

### Sub-phase B5.3 — Never hand him choices while he is travelling
*"Not a bunch of false choices when I wake up in the morning."*

### Sub-phase B5.4 — The retreat ask is the live one
`PO2-13` — the half-day to day of slack. **Time-sensitive, and blocked on him**, because only he can
ask his own partners.

**Exit, receipt:** one real scheduling conflict surfaced and resolved by an advisor without him
adjudicating it.

## THE HONEST SUMMARY OF THIS ROADMAP

Most of R1 through R6 is engineering. **This one is mostly not**, and pretending otherwise would be the
failure.

- **B0 and B1** are drafting and measurement. A coder can do both.
- **B2** is real engineering with a sheet already written.
- **B3** needs his roster and his clicks.
- **B4** is a business decision he has already made and nobody has executed.
- **B5** is his life, and the rule about his life is `PO1-11`: *"Temporary coder, stay out of my
  stuff."* **Advisors do this work. Temp coders build the ground advisors stand on and go no further.**
